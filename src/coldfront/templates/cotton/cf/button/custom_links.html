{% comment %} DaisyUI custom links component — renders non-grouped links as buttons and grouped links as dropdowns. Receives `links` (list of {text, link, new_window, variant, disabled, error}) and `groups` (list of {name, variant, links[]}) from the `custom_links` templatetag. Variants are daisyUI colors (`ColorChoices`). Error links render disabled with a warning icon + tooltip. {% endcomment %}
